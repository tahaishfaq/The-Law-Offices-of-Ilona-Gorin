# Preview changes

The preview is a single homepage. Its interior links lead to the existing website; it does not contain a form or collect leads.

- The call links use the published office number. On the live mobile homepage, a visible link displaying that number was observed pointing to a different number.
- Consultation actions lead to the existing contact page.
- Personal injury and business services have equally prominent entry points.
- The attorney introduction and resources use published material, pending attorney review.
- The preview uses a firm-specific page title and is marked `noindex,nofollow`.

Compare the [existing mobile](screenshots/baseline-mobile.png) and [preview mobile](screenshots/phase-5-mobile.png), or the [existing desktop](screenshots/baseline-desktop.png) and [preview desktop](screenshots/phase-5-desktop.png). Captures were made in Playwright at matched viewport dimensions, but on different local/live environments. The preview captures include Astro's development toolbar; it is absent from the static build.

No speed, search ranking, accessibility conformance or conversion improvement has been measured. See [QA](QA.md) for checks and limitations.
